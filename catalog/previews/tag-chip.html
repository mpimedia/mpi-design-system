TagChip

Category tag chips for CRM contact/account classification. Colour resolves from TagChip::Component::GROUP_VARIANTS as a Bootstrap -subtle/-emphasis pair, so it follows data-bs-theme. The leading dot paints currentColor — inheriting the chip's emphasis foreground — rather than a solid bg-{semantic}, which on a subtle surface would measure only ~2.6:1. Seven categories collapse onto five hues because MPI maps $info → $primary; the text label carries the identity.
